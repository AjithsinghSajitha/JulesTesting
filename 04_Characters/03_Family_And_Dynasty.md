# The Family and Dynasty System

While players can experience countless lives in "Dynasty of the Voiceless," the Family and Dynasty system allows them to build a lasting legacy that persists and grows across generations. This system is the primary long-term goal for many playstyles and is central to the game's identity.

## 1. Courtship and Marriage

NPCs in the world will naturally form relationships, fall in love, and seek to marry based on the "World AI." The player can participate in this system while possessing a character.

*   **Building a Relationship:** Marriage is the culmination of a strong positive relationship. A character must engage in friendly conversations, give gifts, and spend time with a potential partner to build this bond.
*   **Proposing Marriage:** Once the relationship value is high enough, a character can propose. The success of the proposal depends on:
    *   The relationship value.
    *   The social standing of both families. (A noble is unlikely to accept a proposal from a peasant).
    *   The financial stability of the suitor.
    *   The consent of the head of the family (in some cultures, like Alerion's nobility).
*   **Benefits of Marriage:** Marriage forms a formal alliance between two families. It provides social and economic stability and is the primary path to having legitimate children.

## 2. Children and Heirs

Once married, a couple has a chance to have children. This can happen organically for AI-controlled NPCs or as a result of player choice.

*   **Genetics and Inheritance:** A child will inherit a mix of their parents' attributes and traits.
    *   **Attributes (Strength, Intelligence, etc.):** A child's base attributes are an average of their parents' attributes, with a small degree of random variation.
    *   **Traits (Brave, Greedy, etc.):** Children have a high chance of inheriting traits from their parents.
    *   **Appearance:** A child's physical appearance is a procedural blend of their parents' features.
*   **Raising a Child:** Children will grow up in real-time, passing through infancy, childhood, and adolescence before reaching adulthood. During this time, their core personality and skills will be shaped by their parents, their environment, and their own experiences.

## 3. The Dynasty: Playing as Your Heir

This is the core of the dynasty system. When a character the player has possessed has a child, that child becomes a potential new host for the player's "Voiceless Spirit."

*   **Passing the Torch:** At any point after their child reaches adulthood, the player can choose to leave their current host and possess their own child. This allows the player to continue their family's story from a new perspective.
*   **A Living Legacy:** When the player switches to their heir, their previous character becomes an AI-controlled NPC again. They will live out the rest of their life as the parent, offering advice, and continuing to be a part of the family's story.
*   **Generational Gameplay:** This creates a powerful gameplay loop.
    1.  **Generation 1:** The player possesses a poor farmer, works hard to buy a plot of land, gets married, and has a son.
    2.  **Generation 2:** The player possesses the son, who inherits the farm. He uses the family's savings to train as a soldier and becomes a respected guard in the local town. He has a daughter.
    3.  **Generation 3:** The player possesses the daughter. Using her family's reputation and wealth, she enters the world of politics, eventually becoming the mayor of the town her grandfather once farmed outside of.

## 4. Inheritance

When a character dies, their property and titles are passed on to their heirs based on the succession laws of their culture.

*   **Succession Laws:** These vary by faction.
    *   **Kingdom of Alerion:** Primogeniture (oldest child inherits everything).
    *   **Valerium Republic:** Wealth is divided amongst all children, with titles and company ownership often going to the most skilled or favored child.
    *   **Iron Confederacy of Vorstag:** Heirs are chosen based on merit, not just birth order.
*   **Disputes and Drama:** Inheritance is not always smooth. Disinherited children may become rivals. Families may go to war over disputed claims. This system is a major driver of emergent, unscripted conflict in the world.

The Dynasty system transforms the game from a series of individual lives into a grand, overarching story of a single family's rise (or fall) through the ages.
