# Core Gameplay: The RTS Mode ("Overworld View")

The RTS Mode, also known as the "Overworld View," is one of the two primary ways the player interacts with the world of Veridian. This mode is a high-level, strategic interface that allows the player to observe and influence the world on a grand scale.

## 1. Perspective and Control

*   **God-Like View:** The player views the world from a top-down, "god-like" perspective. They can zoom in to see individual towns and armies, or zoom out to see the entire continent.
*   **Indirect Control:** This is a key distinction from traditional RTS games. The player does not directly select and command units. Instead, they influence the world by setting goals, priorities, and policies for the factions and communities they are leading.
*   **The "Hand of God":** The player's cursor is represented as a subtle, ethereal "hand" that can interact with the world in specific ways (e.g., highlighting an area to suggest for settlement, pointing out a resource vein to miners).

## 2. Faction Management

When the player is possessing a faction leader (e.g., a king, a guild master, a chieftain), the RTS mode becomes their primary tool for managing that faction.

*   **Resource Management:** The player can view the faction's income, expenses, and stockpiles of resources (e.g., food, wood, stone, gold). They can set production targets and trade policies. For example, they can issue a decree to "focus on lumber production" or "export surplus grain." The NPCs will then attempt to carry out these orders.
*   **Construction and Development:** Players can designate areas for new buildings, roads, and other infrastructure. They don't place the buildings themselves, but rather create "blueprints" or "zoning" that the NPC builders will then work to complete. The speed and success of construction depend on the availability of resources and skilled labor.
*   **Technology and Research:** The player can direct their faction's research efforts by investing in different technology trees (e.g., military, civic, economic). For example, they could prioritize "improved crop rotation" to boost food production or "stronger steel" for their army.
*   **Diplomacy:** The player can manage relationships with other factions. They can propose treaties, declare war, send gifts, and engage in espionage. The success of these diplomatic actions is determined by a complex system of reputation, faction goals, and the personalities of the leaders involved.

## 3. Army Command

While the player cannot directly control individual soldiers, they can command their armies on a strategic level.

*   **Strategic Objectives:** The player can set high-level objectives for their armies, such as "defend this border," "raid this enemy territory," or "march on the enemy capital."
*   **Formations and Tactics:** Before a battle, the player can set the army's general formation and tactical approach (e.g., "defensive shield wall," "aggressive cavalry charge").
*   **Battle Simulation:** Once two armies meet, the battle is simulated based on a variety of factors, including the number and quality of troops, leadership, terrain, and the chosen tactics. The player can watch the battle unfold in real-time from the Overworld View, but they cannot intervene directly... unless they choose to possess a soldier.

## 4. Information and Observation

Even when not leading a faction, the RTS mode is an invaluable tool for observation.

*   **World Map:** The player can view the entire known world, with detailed information on terrain, biomes, and political boundaries.
*   **Information Overlays:** The player can activate various information overlays, such as a "resource map" showing the location of valuable minerals, or a "diplomatic map" showing the relationships between factions.
*   **Following NPCs:** The player can select any NPC in the world and follow their movements in real-time, providing a powerful tool for understanding the lives and routines of the people they might want to possess.

The RTS Mode is the "brain" of the game, where the player makes the big decisions that shape the world. It is a game of strategy, foresight, and indirect influence, providing a perfect counterpart to the intense, personal action of the Possession Mode.
