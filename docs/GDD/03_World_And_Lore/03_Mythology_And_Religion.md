# Mythology and Religion of Veridian

The belief systems of Veridian are as fractured and diverse as its political landscape. While countless local spirits and folk traditions exist, the most dominant religious framework is the worship of the "Aethelian Pantheon," the gods that rose to prominence during the Age of Heroes.

## The Aethelian Pantheon (The Imperial Faith)

This is the official religion of the Kingdom of Alerion and is widely practiced, in some form, across the continent. The faith is highly organized, with a strict hierarchy of priests and a holy book known as the "Codex of Light."

### The Major Deities

*   **Solara, the Sun-Mother:** The chief deity of the pantheon. She is the goddess of light, life, order, and civilization. She is often depicted as a serene, crowned woman holding a scepter of light. The great cathedrals of the Imperial Faith are built in her honor. Worshipped by rulers, nobles, and judges.
*   **Kael, the Forge-Father:** The husband of Solara. He is the god of creation, craft, strength, and the earth. He is the patron of blacksmiths, artisans, and soldiers. He is depicted as a powerfully built man with a great hammer. Worshipped by common folk, craftsmen, and warriors.
*   **Lumin, the Star-Sailor:** The child of Solara and Kael. Lumin is the god of knowledge, magic, mystery, and the sea. They are a deity of duality, representing both the calm pursuit of wisdom and the chaotic, untamable nature of magic. Patron of scholars, mages, sailors, and explorers. Depicted as an androgynous figure cloaked in stars.

### The Minor Deities

A host of lesser gods and goddesses exist, each governing a specific aspect of life. These include:
*   **Veridia:** Goddess of the harvest and fertility.
*   **Mors:** God of death and the afterlife, seen not as evil but as a necessary guide.
*   **Furtim:** The trickster god of luck, thieves, and merchants.

## Heresies and Folk Religions

While the Imperial Faith is dominant, several other belief systems persist and are often viewed as heresies by the mainstream church.

### The Old Ways
*   **Description:** A collection of ancient, animistic beliefs that predate the Aethelian Empire. Followers of the Old Ways worship the spirits of nature: the ancient trees, the mighty rivers, and the sacred mountains. They do not build temples, but instead gather at natural shrines.
*   **Practitioners:** Common in the rural and wilder parts of Veridian, especially in the Iron Confederacy of Vorstag, where it is quietly tolerated. Druids and shamans are the spiritual leaders of the Old Ways.

### The Cult of the Giant
*   **Description:** Also known as the "Children of the Sundering," this is a dangerous and fanatical cult that worships the long-vanished Giants. They view the Giants as true gods and believe the Aethelian Pantheon to be false idols.
*   **Beliefs:** They believe the Great Sundering was a divine act to punish humanity and that the Giants will one day return to either enslave or destroy mankind. Their rituals are often bloody and involve attempts to awaken the power believed to be dormant in Giant ruins.

### The Philosophy of the Void
*   **Description:** Not a religion in the traditional sense, but a philosophy popular among some intellectuals, atheists, and members of the Argent Hand. It posits that there are no gods and that the universe is governed by chance and the will of mortals.
*   **Beliefs:** Adherents believe that humanity makes its own destiny and that relying on divine intervention is a fool's errand. They see the "Voiceless Spirit" (the player) not as a divine entity, but as a metaphysical anomaly—a source of power to be understood and perhaps even controlled. This view is considered extreme heresy by the Imperial Faith.
