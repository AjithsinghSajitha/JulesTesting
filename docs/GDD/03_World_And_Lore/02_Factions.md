# Factions of Veridian

The continent of Veridian in the Fourth Age is a fractured land, teeming with competing kingdoms, powerful guilds, and zealous religious orders. These are some of the major factions the player will encounter.

## The Successor Kingdoms

These three kingdoms are the primary remnants of the old Aethelian Empire, each claiming to be its true successor. They are locked in a state of cold war, punctuated by frequent border skirmishes.

### 1. The Kingdom of Alerion
*   **Description:** Located in the fertile heartlands of the old Empire, Alerion is the most direct descendant of the Aethelian dynasty. They see themselves as the rightful rulers of all Veridian and are dedicated to reuniting the Empire under their banner, by force if necessary. They are proud, traditional, and have the strongest heavy cavalry in the known world.
*   **Culture:** Feudal society with a strong emphasis on nobility, chivalry, and heraldry. Their architecture is grand and imposing, reflecting their imperial ambitions.
*   **Leader:** King Theron III, an aging but respected monarch who dreams of finishing his grandfather's work of reconquest.

### 2. The Valerium Republic
*   **Description:** Situated along the southern coast, Valerium was once the breadbasket and naval powerhouse of the Empire. After the Shattering, the powerful merchant families and naval admirals overthrew the imperial governor and established a republic. They value wealth, trade, and naval supremacy above all else.
*   **Culture:** A bustling, cosmopolitan society driven by commerce. Their cities are vibrant and wealthy, filled with markets from across the world. They have a powerful navy and rely on well-paid mercenary armies for land defense.
*   **Leader:** The Republic is governed by a council of the five wealthiest merchant-princes. The current elected head of the council is the shrewd and ruthless Lady Arabelle.

### 3. The Iron Confederacy of Vorstag
*   **Description:** Forged in the cold and mountainous north, Vorstag was a rebellious frontier province of the Empire, valued for its rich iron and coal deposits. The people of Vorstag are hardy, pragmatic, and fiercely independent. They have little interest in conquering the south, but will defend their sovereignty to the last man.
*   **Culture:** A stoic and industrious people who value strength and engineering. Their armies are known for their heavily armored infantry, masterful siege engines, and innovative use of early black powder weapons.
*   **Leader:** The Confederacy is led by an elected Lord-General, a position currently held by the grizzled veteran Kaelen "Ironhand."

## Minor Factions & Guilds

These organizations operate across the continent, holding significant power that can rival that of the major kingdoms.

### The Argent Hand
*   **Description:** A secretive and influential organization of bankers, spies, and information brokers. The Argent Hand has no army, but their wealth can fund kingdoms, and their information can topple them. They operate out of a network of hidden offices in every major city.
*   **Goal:** To maintain a balance of power in Veridian, ensuring no single kingdom becomes powerful enough to threaten their business. They profit from chaos but fear total war.

### The Circle of Scribes
*   **Description:** A neutral order dedicated to the preservation of knowledge from the Age of Heroes. They reside in secluded monasteries and libraries, copying ancient texts and safeguarding lost technologies. They are respected by all factions but are fiercely protective of their secrets.
*   **Goal:** To prevent the complete loss of the old Empire's knowledge and to wait for a time when the world is ready for its return.

### The Children of the Sundering
*   **Description:** A fanatical religious cult that worships the ancient Giants. They believe the Great Sundering was a divine punishment for humanity's arrogance and that the Giants will one day return to cleanse the world. They are often found in remote areas, excavating Giant ruins and performing strange rituals.
*   **Goal:** To hasten the return of the Giants, often through violent and destructive means. They are universally feared and hunted by the major kingdoms.
