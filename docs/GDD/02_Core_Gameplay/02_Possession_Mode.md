# Core Gameplay: The Possession Mode

The Possession Mode is the heart and soul of "Dynasty of the Voiceless." It is where the grand strategy of the RTS mode gives way to the intimate, personal experience of living a single life in the world of Veridian.

## 1. The Act of Possession

*   **Entering Possession:** From the Overworld View, the player can select any living, non-player character (NPC) and choose to possess them. The camera then seamlessly zooms from the sky down into the first-person perspective of the chosen NPC.
*   **The "Voiceless" Spirit:** The player is a disembodied spirit. When they possess an NPC, they gain full control over their body and actions. However, the NPC's mind, personality, and skills remain. The player can hear the NPC's thoughts as a form of internal monologue, providing guidance and context for their life and relationships.
*   **Leaving Possession:** The player can choose to leave their host at any time, returning to the disembodied Overworld View. When they do, the NPC's own AI will resume control, and they will continue with their life, retaining the memories and consequences of the player's actions.

## 2. First-Person Gameplay

Once in Possession Mode, the game plays like a first-person action/RPG.

*   **Direct Control:** The player has direct, real-time control over the character's movement, actions, and interactions.
*   **Skill-Based Actions:** The success of most actions is determined by a combination of the player's skill and the possessed NPC's own abilities.
    *   **Combat:** Combat is a visceral, skill-based affair. If you possess a trained knight, you will have access to their advanced combat techniques. If you possess a simple farmer, you will have to rely on your wits and whatever makeshift weapon you can find.
    *   **Crafting:** Crafting systems (e.g., blacksmithing, alchemy, cooking) are interactive mini-games. A skilled blacksmith will have better timing and more options in the blacksmithing mini-game.
    *   **Social Interaction:** Dialogue with other NPCs is handled through a dynamic conversation system. The player chooses the intent of their response (e.g., friendly, deceptive, threatening), and the actual words are determined by the possessed NPC's personality and relationship with the other character.
*   **Needs and Survival:** While possessing an NPC, the player must manage their basic needs, such as hunger, thirst, and fatigue.

## 3. Living a Life

The core of the Possession Mode is about experiencing the world from a grounded, personal perspective.

*   **Jobs and Economy:** Every NPC has a job, from farming and mining to ruling a kingdom. The player can perform this job to earn money, gain resources, and contribute to the local economy. Excelling at a job can lead to promotions and new opportunities.
*   **Relationships and Family:** The player can build relationships with other NPCs. They can make friends, find rivals, get married, and even have children. These relationships are persistent and will evolve over time.
*   **Quests and Opportunities:** Quests are not a predefined list of tasks. They emerge organically from the needs of the world. A food shortage might create a "quest" to hunt for more game. A bandit attack might create an "opportunity" to defend the village. The player discovers these needs by talking to people and observing the world around them.

## 4. Impact on the World

The actions taken in Possession Mode have a direct and lasting impact on the larger world.

*   **Bottom-Up Change:** While the RTS mode is about top-down influence, the Possession Mode is about bottom-up change. A player who possesses a miner and discovers a new vein of gold can trigger a gold rush that transforms a sleepy village into a bustling city.
*   **Turning the Tide of Battle:** In a large-scale battle planned from the RTS view, the player can possess a single soldier. By successfully completing a heroic action—like slaying an enemy commander or capturing a key position—they can boost the morale of their allies and turn the tide of the entire battle.
*   **A Personal Legacy:** If a player has a child while possessing an NPC, that child becomes a new, unique NPC in the world. The player can then choose to possess that child later in life, continuing their family's story and building a dynasty that can last for generations. This is the ultimate expression of the game's core fantasy.
