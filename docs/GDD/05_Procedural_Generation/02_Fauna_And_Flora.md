# Procedural Generation: Fauna and Flora

Once the terrain and biomes of Veridian are generated, the world is populated with a diverse array of plant and animal life. This process is not random; it is governed by ecological rules that ensure each biome has a distinct and believable ecosystem.

## 1. Flora (Plant Life)

The type of vegetation that grows in a region is determined by the biome. The system uses procedural placement algorithms to scatter trees, shrubs, and collectible herbs in a natural-looking way.

*   **Forests:**
    *   **Temperate Forests:** Populated with oak, maple, and birch trees. The undergrowth contains ferns, mosses, and common herbs like Starthistle (a healing agent).
    *   **Taiga (Boreal Forests):** Dominated by coniferous trees like pine, fir, and spruce. The ground is covered in pine needles and hardy shrubs. Fire-resistant Ironwood trees can be found here.
    *   **Tropical Rainforests:** Dense canopy of massive, exotic trees. The forest floor is a tangle of vines, giant ferns, and rare, brightly-colored flowers used in potent alchemy (e.g., Sun-Kissed Orchids).
*   **Grasslands and Plains:** Wide-open spaces covered in different types of grasses and wildflowers. Trees are sparse, typically found near water sources. Home to vast fields of wheat-like grains that can be domesticated.
*   **Deserts:** Sparse vegetation, including cacti, hardy shrubs, and the rare Desert Rose, a crystal-like flower that blooms only at night.
*   **Tundra and Mountains:** Only the hardiest plants survive here, such as lichen, mosses, and the resilient Frost-Lichen, an ingredient for cold-resistance potions.

## 2. Fauna (Animal Life)

Animals are placed in the world based on their biome, their position in the food chain, and their behaviors. Each species has its own AI that governs its daily cycle of hunting, grazing, and sleeping.

### Common Wildlife (Prey and Passive Creatures)

These animals form the foundation of the ecosystem and are a common source of food and materials for NPCs and the player.

*   **Deer and Elk:** Graze in forests and grasslands. They are timid and will flee from predators and humanoids.
*   **Wild Boar:** Found in forests. They are aggressive if cornered.
*   **Rabbits and Foxes:** Common in nearly all but the most extreme environments.
*   **Mountain Goats:** Sure-footed creatures found in high-altitude, rocky terrain.
*   **Giant Tortoises:** Slow-moving, passive herbivores found in temperate and tropical regions. Their shells are a valuable resource.

### Predators

These animals hunt other wildlife and can pose a threat to unwary travelers.

*   **Wolves:** Hunt in packs in forests and tundra. They are intelligent and will try to surround their prey.
*   **Bears:** Solitary and powerful predators found in mountains and forests. Highly territorial.
*   **Sabre-Toothed Cats:** Fast and deadly hunters that stalk the grasslands and jungles.
*   **Giant Snakes:** Dwell in swamps and rainforests, using camouflage to ambush their prey.

### Unique and Magical Creatures (The "Monsters")

These rare and dangerous creatures are the stuff of legend. They often guard valuable resources or ancient ruins. Encounters with them are meant to be memorable and challenging.

*   **Gryphons:** Majestic beasts with the body of a lion and the head and wings of an eagle. They build their nests on high mountain peaks and are fiercely territorial. Their feathers are prized by fletchers.
*   **Rock Golems:** Elemental spirits that have animated a body of stone. They are dormant and indistinguishable from large boulders until disturbed. Found in areas of high magical energy or around Giant ruins.
*   **Wyverns:** Vicious, two-legged draconic cousins. They are smaller and less intelligent than true dragons but are still a significant threat. They make their lairs in volcanic regions and are known to raid livestock (and people).
*   **The Gloomwood Stalker:** A terrifying, shadowy beast that hunts only in the oldest and darkest forests. It is a creature of pure nightmare, known for its ability to mimic sounds and create illusions to lure its victims. Its existence is often dismissed as a campfire story.

This procedural placement of flora and fauna ensures that the world of Veridian is not just a landscape, but a living, breathing ecosystem full of resources to gather, animals to hunt, and monsters to fear.
